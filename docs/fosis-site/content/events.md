What’s On

## FOSIS Events Across the UK

FOSIS runs over 100 national and regional events each year, from large-scale conferences to intimate training days, socials, dinners, sports tournaments, and Islamic camps. Our events bring Muslim students together from every corner of the UK and Ireland, building the connections and community that define the FOSIS experience.

Types of Events

## Something for Every Student

#### Careers & Professional

The Ihsaan National Careers Conference Series, employer networking events, and professional development workshops across London, the Midlands, and the North.

#### Faith & Da’wah

Discover Islam Week, national speaker tours featuring world-class Islamic scholars, Da’wah training days, and spiritual development retreats and camps.

#### Community & Social

Regional dinners, sports tournaments, charity fundraisers including the national Charity Week for Orphans, and community gatherings that bring students together beyond the campus.

Follow **@fosischannel** on Instagram and X to stay up to date with upcoming events. If you’d like to host or partner on a FOSIS event at your university, get in touch at **[info@fosis.org.uk](mailto:info@fosis.org.uk)**.
