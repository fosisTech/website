# Book Islamophobia Training

`[marquee ticker]` Est. 1963 · 350,000 Students · 100+ Affiliated ISocs · UK & Ireland

For Student Unions

## Making Campuses Safer for Muslim Students

Islamophobia is a lived reality for many Muslim students across UK campuses. FOSIS delivers a comprehensive, interactive Islamophobia Awareness Training programme specifically designed for Student Union leaders, sabbatical officers, and university staff, equipping them with the knowledge, language, and tools to better support Muslim students and drive institutional change.

> **“When Student Unions are formally trained in understanding Islamophobia and the experiences of Muslim students, it makes them feel safer, heard, and represented.”** FOSIS

---

The Training

## What the Session Covers

### 01

#### Defining Islamophobia

A rigorous examination of what Islamophobia is, how it manifests in educational settings, and why the APPG definition matters for institutional policy.

### 02

#### Student Experiences

Real accounts of how Islamophobia affects Muslim students, from micro-aggressions to structural discrimination, helping leaders develop genuine empathy and awareness.

### 03

#### Institutional Response

Practical steps Student Unions and universities can take, from policy reform to complaints procedures, to create genuinely inclusive campuses for Muslim students.

### 04

#### Prevent & Policy

An examination of the Prevent strategy’s impact on Muslim students and how Student Unions can advocate for students navigating counter-terrorism policies on campus.

---

Who It’s For

### Designed for Student Union Leaders

The training is tailored for sabbatical officers, welfare staff, diversity leads, and anyone in a student union who works to support Muslim students. Sessions are interactive, evidence-based, and delivered by FOSIS representatives with direct experience of Muslim student welfare.
