FOSIS Careers Programme

## What is Ihsaan?

Ihsaan is FOSIS’s national careers conference series, a programme built around a single conviction: that every Muslim student in the UK and Ireland deserves the support and resources to enter their dream career and, in doing so, build a more ethical, God-conscious society for all.

The name *Ihsaan* – meaning excellence and virtue in Arabic – reflects the spirit of the programme: not just securing a job, but pursuing a career with purpose, integrity, and lasting impact. Supported by My Fridays, Ihsaan brings together students, professionals, and employers across the UK in a series of regional career conferences each year.

## A Conference Built for Muslim Students

01

#### Expert-Led Talks

Industry leaders and professionals share their journeys, insights, and practical guidance across sectors including law, finance, medicine, tech, and more.

02

#### Employer Stalls

Connect directly with employers, graduate schemes, and organisations actively seeking Muslim talent. Bring your CV, ask questions, and make the connections that matter.

## Coming to a City Near You

Ihsaan runs as a series of regional conferences across the UK, making career support accessible to Muslim students wherever they study. Past events have been hosted at UCL (London), the University of Birmingham (Midlands), and the University of Manchester (North), with each conference tailored to the specific student communities in that region.

#### 🏙 London

Hosted in partnership with UCL Islamic Society

#### 🏭 Midlands

Hosted in partnership with University of Birmingham ISoc

#### 🌿 North

Hosted in partnership with University of Manchester ISoc

Keep an eye on our Events page and follow **@ihsaan.fosis** on Instagram for announcements about upcoming conference dates and how to register.
