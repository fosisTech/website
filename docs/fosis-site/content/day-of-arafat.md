# The Day of *Arafat*

A gentle, gathered space to prepare for, and walk through, the most blessed day of the year. Take what nourishes you, leave what doesn't.

🚨

Disclaimer.

This is just for inspiration, don't feel pressured to do everything. Focus on what feels right for you and adjust as needed. Do what helps you connect with Allah, and don't compare yourself to others. Just try your best 💪

💡

How to use this page.

Tick items off as you move through the day, the progress bar at the top will fill as you go. Try not to refresh the page once you've started, so you don't lose your place.

## Virtues of the Day

✦

### The day the religion was perfected

In Al-Sahihayn it was reported from 'Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA) that a Jewish man said to him, "O Amir al-Muminin, there is a verse in your Book which you recite; if it had come to us Jews, we would have taken that day as an 'Eid (festival)."

'Umar said, "Which verse?" He said:

> "This day I have perfected your religion for you, completed My favour upon you, and have chosen for you Islam as your religion."al-Maidah 5:3 (interpretation of the meaning)

'Umar said, "We know on which day and in which place that was revealed to the Prophet ﷺ. It was when he was standing in 'Arafah on a Friday."

☼

### A day by which Allah swore an oath

> "By the witnessing day [Friday] and by the witnessed day [the Day of 'Arafah]."al-Buruj 85:3

Abu Hurayrah (RA) reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: *"The promised day is the Day of Resurrection, the witnessed day is the Day of 'Arafah, and the witnessing day is Friday."*

Narrated by al-Tirmidhi and classed as sahih by al-Albani

⟁

### The covenant from the progeny of Adam

Ibn 'Abbas (RA) said: the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: *"Allah took the covenant from the loins of Adam in Na'man, i.e., 'Arafah. He brought forth from his loins all his offspring and spread them before Him, then He addressed them, and said: Am I not your Lord? They said, 'Yes, we testify,' lest you should say on the Day of Resurrection: 'Verily, we have been unaware of this.'"*

> "…Or lest you should say: 'It was only our fathers aforetime who took others as partners in worship along with Allah, and we were (merely their) descendents after them; will You then destroy us because of the deeds of men who practiced Al-Batil…?'"al-A'raf 7:172–173

Narrated by Ahmad and classed as sahih by al-Albani. **And there is no greater day than this, and no greater covenant than this.**

♡

### Forgiveness, freedom from the Fire

In Sahih Muslim it was narrated from 'Aishah (RA) that the Prophet ﷺ said: *"There is no day on which Allah frees more people from the Fire than the Day of 'Arafah. He comes close and expresses His pride to the angels, saying, 'What do these people want?'"*

Ibn 'Umar narrated that the Prophet ﷺ said: *"Allah expresses His pride to His angels at the last part of the day on the Day of 'Arafah, about the people of 'Arafah. He says, 'Look at My slaves who have come unkempt and dusty.'"*

Narrated by Ahmad and classed as sahih by al-Albani

𓂀

### Fasting expiates two years of sins

Abu Qatadah (RA) narrated that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ was asked about fasting on the Day of 'Arafah. He said:

> "It expiates for the sins of the previous year and of the coming year."Narrated by Muslim

**This (fasting) is recommended for those who are not on Hajj.**

## Preparing for the Day

### In the days leading up

Small steps to soften the heart before the day arrives.

- [ ] Seek forgiveness and tawbah for sins that you have committed

- [ ] Message people you may have wronged asking for their forgiveness

- [ ] Make sincere dua that Allah allows you to make the most of this day

- [ ] Make a dua list, include every little thing you want, so nothing is forgotten

### Clear the deck

Complete any outstanding tasks so you are not burdened with them during the day of Arafat.

## A Suggested Timetable

🌙

### The night before

Isha → Fajr

- [ ] Pray Isha and go straight to sleep

- [ ] Wake up for Tahajjud

- [ ] Suhoor if you will be fasting

🌅

### Fajr to Zuhr

Dawn → Midday

- [ ] Pray Fajr

- [ ] Adhkar and takbeerat after Fajr

- [ ] Morning adhkar

- [ ] Recite Qur'an

- [ ] Stay awake until sunrise if possible

- [ ] Pray 4 rakah of salat at Duha

- [ ] Pray Zuhr

- [ ] Adhkar and takbeerat after Zuhr

- [ ] Donate to charity (see Useful Links)

🌇

### Zuhr to Maghrib

Midday → Sunset

Between Zuhr and Maghrib is a time of great acceptance, make dua for everything you want.

- [ ] Make dua for everything you want (time of great acceptance)

- [ ] Alternate between acts of worship

- [ ] Pray Asr

- [ ] Adhkar and takbeerat after Asr

- [ ] Evening adhkar

- [ ] Intensify dua in the final hour before Maghrib

🌃

### Maghrib & beyond

Sunset onward

- [ ] Break your fast with sincerity and gratitude

- [ ] Pray Maghrib

- [ ] Adhkar and takbeerat after Maghrib

- [ ] Pray Isha

- [ ] Reflect on the day, thank Allah, and rest
