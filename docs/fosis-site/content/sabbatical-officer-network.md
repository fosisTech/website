# Sabbatical Officer Network

`[marquee ticker]` Est. 1963 · 350,000 Students · 100+ Affiliated ISocs · UK & Ireland

For Student Unions

## Muslim Students Are Shaping Student Unions

## 53

Muslim Sabbatical Officers

Elected across student unions nationwide in a single year, an unprecedented milestone showing the growing influence of Muslim students in representative politics.

The FOSIS Sabbatical Officer Network exists to connect, support, and empower Muslim students serving as elected officers in student unions across the UK and Ireland. For many Muslim sabbatical officers, navigating the intersection of their faith, their community, and institutional student union culture can be isolating. This network changes that.

Through peer connection, shared resources, and FOSIS support, members of the network can advocate more effectively for Muslim students at their institutions while drawing on a national community of officers who understand their unique position.

---

What the Network Offers

## Support Designed Around You

#### Peer Community

Connect with Muslim sabbatical officers from across the UK, people who understand the unique dynamics of being a Muslim in a student union leadership role.

#### FOSIS Backing

Direct access to FOSIS’s national advocacy team for guidance on Muslim student welfare cases, Islamophobia incidents, and navigating institutional policies including Prevent.

#### Training & Resources

Access tailored resources, policy briefings, and training sessions to help you represent Muslim students more effectively within your student union.
