# About FOSIS

The Federation of Student Islamic Societies, the national voice for Muslim students across the UK & Ireland since 1963.

`[marquee ticker]` Est. 1963 · 350,000 Students · 100+ Affiliated ISocs · UK & Ireland

60+ Years of Impact

Our Mission

## Serving Muslim Students Since 1963

FOSIS was founded in 1963 with a single purpose: to unite Muslim students across the United Kingdom and ensure their voice was heard. Over six decades later, we remain the UK’s largest Muslim student organisation, connecting over 150 Islamic Societies and representing more than 350,000 students.

From spiritual development to career guidance, from national advocacy to grassroots community building, we are the infrastructure behind Muslim student excellence.

Our Story

## A Legacy of Leadership

From the very first national gathering of Muslim students in 1963, FOSIS has shaped the landscape of student Islamic life. We have championed the right to pray on campus, fought Islamophobia at every level, and built the frameworks that empower ISoc leaders to run thriving communities.

Every generation of Muslim students has left its mark on FOSIS, and FOSIS has left its mark on every generation of Muslim students.
