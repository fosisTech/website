# FOSIS Report

A reporting portal for ISocs and Muslim students to submit incidents regarding ISoc issues, SU problems, or Islamophobia.

`[marquee ticker]` Est. 1963 · 350,000 Students · 100+ Affiliated ISocs · UK & Ireland

The FOSIS Report is a confidential reporting portal for ISocs and Muslim students across the UK and Ireland. If you have experienced or witnessed issues relating to your Islamic Society, your Students’ Union, or Islamophobia on campus, this is where you can tell us about it.

Reports submitted through this portal are reviewed by FOSIS’s national team. Depending on the nature of the incident, we may follow up directly, escalate to the relevant university or SU, or incorporate findings into our national advocacy work and annual report.

## What Can I Report?

This portal is for reporting three categories of incident:

🕌

ISoc Issues

Internal Society Concerns

Disputes within your ISoc, committee misconduct, governance failures, or issues with how your society is being run.

🏛️

SU Problems

Students’ Union Concerns

Unfair treatment by your Students’ Union, barriers to ISoc recognition or funding, or SU policies that disproportionately affect Muslim students.

⚠️

Islamophobia

On-Campus Incidents

Verbal abuse, discrimination, microaggressions, or structural Islamophobia experienced or witnessed on campus or in university settings.

## What Happens Next?

Once your report is received, our team will review it and determine the appropriate response. This may include direct follow-up with you (if you provided contact details), liaison with your university or SU, formal escalation through our advocacy channels, or inclusion in our anonymised national data to support systemic change.

If your situation is urgent or you require immediate support, please contact us directly at [student.affairs@fosis.org.uk](mailto:student.affairs@fosis.org.uk) or reach out to your [Regional Chair](https://fosis.org.uk/about-us/).
