## Affiliate Your ISoc with FOSIS

Affiliating with FOSIS means joining a national network that has been serving Muslim students since 1963. As an affiliated ISoc, your society gains direct access to FOSIS’s full suite of services, representation on a national level, and the collective strength of over 150 Islamic Societies working together across the UK and Ireland.

---

What You Get

## Benefits of Affiliation

#### National Representation

Your ISoc’s voice becomes part of a national conversation. FOSIS advocates for affiliated societies at university, government, and media level.

#### Training & Support

Access our full committee training programme, Da’wah training, leadership development workshops, and ongoing advisory support throughout the year.

#### Freshers Packs

Receive thousands of FOSIS freshers packs each year to distribute at your Freshers Fairs, a proven tool for welcoming and connecting new Muslim students to your ISoc.
